---
title: Letter from Olive to Karl Wolfe
layout: post
date: September 9, 1930
---
<u>2</u>

Thank you for the cards, I love the Rodins. You know what David Copperfield said??? Would you like [its? the?] [Memluay's?] back? You probably need them more than I do. Is not the cathedral restored yet? 

The invitation is open as long as you like, I shall expect you on the way home from Italy. Spain is wonderful, you must fit it in. Would you like a few Spanish swearwords? Geoffrey and I specialize in them, [they?] are warrented to make an spaniard run his knife into you......

If you go to Azay, tell me how Toby really is, we have done our best to find him a good eye doctor in Berlin. Also I wrote to Berlin to a doctor there, to know the best, I'll let him know as soon as I get the answer, tomorrow or the next day.

I rather thought you would like flowers, as soon as you are safely in Italy and settled down, you can have some mauve Scottish and white Sussex heather. It is supposed to be lucky.

Oh Jack, I am afraid of what you have asked me to do, are you sure you don't mind? I am sure I am not gentle enough, and am very afraid to touch on sacred ground, I'd hate you [?] and hurt and lock me [?] [?AST"] together on it.

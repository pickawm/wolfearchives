69 Chatsworth Rd.

London. N.W.2.

9/9/30

Dear Jack,

I have delayed longer than I thought to have done over a letter that needed much an immediate reply. Unfortunately my Mother is pretty ill again, and most things at home depend on me, not the housework, but cooking and ordering and seeing that Mother rests enough, besides her far-too-many outside interests which have all appeared again. Next week we are all going to Sussex for two weeks, Daddie is very tired, and Geoffrey, just back from [Weisbaden?], does not start school for a fortnight. It's going to be glorious there [?] [iveatter?] is fine and all the fruit [?] in the garden [?]

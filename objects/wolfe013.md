---
title: Letter from Olive to Karl Wolfe
layout: post
date: November 1, 1930
---

porch was not out, otherwise I would have sent you one of that too. I do hope you were in Paris to believe it. You know dried lavender keeps its smell over a year when simply kept out of water and dried? 

I have kept you long enough, if this letter crosses one of yours saying you had the flowers, I am glad, because then you will have to write again! (now I <u>am</u> a "nasty little cat" as Daddie puts it), but I look forward to the mail from France. Even Madame [Ganyon's?] spidery French scratch which come very often.

If you are in Paris remember me to the garcon of course, and Andree. If you are in Azay-le-Rideau, [?] to Toby, also of course, lots for yourself. 

Yours ever,

<u>Olive</u>

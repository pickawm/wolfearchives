---
title: Letter from Olive to Karl Wolfe
layout: post
date: November 1, 1930
---

4

ago I met them by chance in Berlin. I have seldom known anyone as lovable as that dean Professor, and today I read in the paper that "Professor [Bakerwald?] died yesterday in Haifa" Just that, it's horrid, he was only fifty and was hard at work designing the Jordan Irrigation Works. They called me 'daughter-in-law and gave me a lovely ring to make it ring true, as they never had any children of their own. 
I am sorry, it is absurd writing this on paper. I suppose all these things, losing friends and finding jobs and being happy or sad are character-forming or some such [?]
Can you get English or American newspapers where you are? [Selpirolyes?] sell every paper in the world, so I [?] send you some if you like. Only [?] I know about [?]

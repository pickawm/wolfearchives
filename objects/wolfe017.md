---
title: Letter from Olive to Karl Wolfe
layout: post
date: September 9, 1930
---

She was having a wonderful time with two nice Englishmen, one of whom is a family friend. She sends her regards, I only [knew?] her for half an hour, the American habit of doing London called too strongly!

Next time don't bother whether what you write is English or not if it is about you [in?] double-Dutch I like it. 

Yours ever,

<u>Olive</u>

The Guy? And my bookplate? I like Toby a lot.

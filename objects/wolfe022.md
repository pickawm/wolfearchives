---
title: Letter from Thomas Jefferson "Toby" Tobias to Karl Wolfe
layout: post
date: January 2, 1938
---

127 East Bay Street, 

Charleston, South Carolina


January 2, 1938

Dear Jack:

Your Christmas card reach me over here in Charleston where I have been for almost three years. It brought back all sorts of pleasant memories of our stay in Europe. How I wish you were here to talk things over!

Considerable water has passed under the bridge with me to say the least. I have been married for something over three years, have a little daughter, Judith, age 11 months, and have another baby ( a boy, I hope) on the way.

What in the hell has been happening to you? When are you coming over to Charleston to paint some portraits? 

Drop me a line and let me know what's been happening to you. God knows whether this letter will reach you as I wrote about a year or so ago to you simply addressing Jackson, Miss., and it came back.

Best regards and hoping to hear from you soon,

Toby

<u>3</u>

I must answer your letter, have you gone to Azay-le-Rideau?
No Jack, I should hate you if you hadn't got that "cavity of affection" which needs filling, it is the essence of you, don't you dare get "hard or isolated". I don't think you need ever feel like that whilst Toby is in his right mind and whilst I am in existence. We proved that, didn't we?

<u>Next point.</u> Since I've left, you seem to have seen the things I wanted to see, no [?] presume you had the fine weather I longed for. There is something you can do for me. Though, [?] you get me two cards of those lovely [?] cathedrals in the Louvre amongst the modern [?]. Next to the Degas picture I think. A[?] would like some cards of the Rodin [?] [?] the choice to you. Will you [?]

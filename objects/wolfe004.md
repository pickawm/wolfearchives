title: Letter from Sam Gore to Karl Wolfe
layout: post
date: February 1, 1983
---
2-1-83

Dear Karl,

It was very considerate of you to send the clipping and I want you to know that I appreciate it. I have a lot of repsect for you and Mildred and your work.

I seem to stay over-committed in my work and don't know what it means to be "caught up." I will, with your permission and when I can sneak away, stop by for a brief visit.

Sincerely,

Sam Gore

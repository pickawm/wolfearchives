---
title: Letter from Olive to Karl Wolfe
layout: post
date: October 27, 1930
---

<u>5</u>

Toby, too shy to come, and too distrustful of the world in general to believe that you are really wanted and your prescence could be honestly welcomed by a family you don't know. Am I right? That is why I would so enjoy spoiling you for a week, my own faith in the world's goodness is infinite, it might not be when I am a spinster of sixty, but at present I have more than enough. You may have a dose of it whenever you like!

Next point. The Guy. Is he finished? Is the composition good?? Did the lady have a baby as the garcon said she would??? Maybe the baby won't fit in the picture and was murdered at birth????

Again, Next point. Yes I am nearly quite [?] thank you, I do nothing but feed it [?] the family calls "good [?]"

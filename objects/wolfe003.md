date: May 19, 1969
---

in Jackson and want to be sure she reads. She is an only child but very fine. Did work in Miss Bank + Trust Co but now home rearing her first child a little girl just two months old. She loves the business world but guess that is natural. I worked all my life in office work first at Searey Store in Bhaven then fourteen yrs at McGraths in office. And Devrey was and is office worker. We both are retired but he is tax practitioning and works at home. We have several hundred tax customers and aside from money earned we love people and enjoy seeing our friends!

My next trip to Jackson will call you. So glad you wrote the article and had published. Keep up the good work and I shall be looking forward to reading more. May God bless you and yours.

Sincerely, Aileen M. Gwin

Mrs. Devrey Devin

Boguechitto, 39629

Miss

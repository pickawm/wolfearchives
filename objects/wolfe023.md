761 West Peachtree

Atlanta, Georgia

Saturday

[?]r letter I have come on over here to Atlanta where I managed to [?]orial job on Mr. Hearst's Georgian American by a stroke of luck from [?] on high as the newspaper racket is in lousy shape just now along with [steel?] and ladies underwear.

[Got?] a letter from Jennings Perry the other day. He is back in New York, at 148 Waverly Place, banging out yams for the pulp magazines about tall fair women and strong hairy chested men. It was good to hear from that old scoundrel. I like his easy acceptance of the good things of life: women, wine, talk, and friends.... His philosophy is pleasantly the opposite of complexity.

There is much heat and many coca-colas to let me know that summer holds sway. As I marked time in Paris by the occasions when I put off and pulled on pajamas, so time in Atlanta is reckoned by the visits of Johnnie, the coca-cola supply boy of the office.

It's a [excellent?] though academic [study?] of the romantic philosophy with which we both agreed we were saturated in Paris.

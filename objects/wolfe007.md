for any length of time. Introspection is a horrid thing and only makes one miserable, at least it does me. 

<u>Next point</u>. I don't mind about the painting of me, they won't like it I know, and they like the little sketch so much, Daddie says the big [one?] is a portrait of a "nasty little cat!"

<u>Next point</u>. (all these are from your letter.) I am so happy that you are coming over here for a week before Italy, (?) Mumsie knows Toby and wants to meet the other boy who was so good to me when I knew hardly anyone in Paris. You must be prepared to be really spoilt and petted, I think you need it. If you come quite soon you will see more of me, on the other hand if you come later I shall probably be one of the world's workers, but you will [?] clever and amusing young brother [?]

<u>6</u>

not me but clothes, thank the Lord. 

Next and last point, you ask me if I will write to you again, you know I will, if it gives you pleasure. That is selfish of me too, because I have the family failing of enjoying writing letters. Writing is an old weakness of this small family, if we aren't jolly careful, Godfrey will turn into a writer instead of a sensible lawyer or doctor. There are too many scribbles already.

Did you get the lavender? I have not yet got over the shock of you asking me what it looked like. When I went to the cottage for the day I brought back an armful of flowers and posted some to Madame Ganyon as well. She also had never seen it.

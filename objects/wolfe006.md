<u>2</u>

a half before I have to catch the last [peek?].

I expect you have gathered by now that I have been left in charge at home, my parents are at the cottage. Our [wish?] maid is on holidays, and it is a good that that I can, and like the cook, because the [daily] maid can only boil beef and fry eggs, the true tasteless fashion that prevails in England. My grandparents are used to French cooking. They are perfectly [weak?] and spoil me all they can, my only trouble is [that?] I get no work done of my own, but I suppose this is good for me too. 

What do you think of the photos? I think they are [topping?] and love to look at them. Did you know I had taken you? [?] wouldn't let me and I did want one. [?] good? Please send me [?]

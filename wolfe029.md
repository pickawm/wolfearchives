title: Letter from Thomas Jefferson "Toby" Tobias to Karl Wolfe
layout: post
date: September 19, 1930
---

[Mon?] Vieux,

The Gods have chosen to make me the victim of their whimsical hilarity, after years of believing that slowly my sight was vanishing [+ that?] like Mitton, I would soon only sit + wait, all is suddenly changed by the announcement of a certain rolly-polly German eye specialist, Dr. [Kreuekmann?], by name who clearly informed me after an examination that there was not a damn thing wrong with my eye. He picked up my spectacles -- [those?] same which I had you include in my bookplate, so sure was I of their permanance on my nose -- are what the eminent doctor calls "feuster glas" {translation: window glass}. He tells me that if it amuses me to wear them; they do no harm. The tiny trace of astigmatism in my left eye is not even worth glasses.

Since Monday I have done without my glasses + find myself none the worst! The Herr doctor seems to think the whole business due to a psychological condition. He told me that he would not risk his reputation by telling me I have nothing the matter with my optics if there actually was something wrong. He suggetsed that I consult a specialist on the nasal tract to see if there was any explaination for my headaches about + between my eyes. I have done so, + the only thing discovered has been a slight cloudyness in the left sinus region. 

I am giving this very revolutionary idea a careful try out for a couple of weeks. I'll be damned if I know what to think about the matter, or if what I <u>do</u> think is worth a damn. after what his doctor has said (+ I feel his sincerity his reputation, his increduality, my landlady tells me, is the best in Germany) when my  eyes troble me + don't know whether they actually hurt or if its just another one of my ideas! It's a most humiliating position, mon vieux. 

My landlady is perfectly swell. She [?] went with me to see the eye doctor, did all the translating, explained that I was a poor student (so he charged me only 20 marks -$5-) + generally looked after me. Anne found the "new" Berlin very much on the American plan, with automats, Quick Lunches, Photomations, etc. Kaiser Fredrich [?useum] is the [nuts?] for Hals + Reubrandt, [?] German lessons from a lady of the [?] now very poor -- Auf wiedersehen

<u>P.S.</u> I have left these borders very wide in case you want to write some <u>letters</u> home, or a novel, or something. -- Incidentally, have [chopped?] the [board?] after having pictures taken in a [Photomation?]. The razor wept, but I had no pity. Shall send a few hairs of it to Olive, + other friends. -- [B?] to Pollak. Luck on the Spanish expedition.
